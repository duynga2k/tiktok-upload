Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 8, 2026

1. Overview

TikTok Upload is a desktop application that enables authorized users to upload and publish their own videos to TikTok using TikTok's official APIs.

2. TikTok Account Information

When a user connects a TikTok account, the application may access basic account information made available through the permissions authorized by the user.

3. Authentication

Authentication is performed using TikTok's official OAuth process. TikTok Upload does not request or store the user's TikTok password.

4. Video Content

The application processes video files selected by the user for the purpose of uploading or publishing them to the user's authorized TikTok account.

5. How Information Is Used

Information obtained through TikTok is used only to provide the authentication and content publishing functionality requested by the user.

6. Data Sharing

TikTok Upload does not sell users' personal information. Information is transmitted to TikTok when necessary to perform user-requested TikTok API operations.

7. Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect authentication credentials and other application data.

8. User Control

Users may disconnect or revoke the application's access to their TikTok account through the account and authorization controls provided by TikTok.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect changes to the application or its data practices.

10. Contact

Questions regarding this Privacy Policy can be directed to the application operator.
